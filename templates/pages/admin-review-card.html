On the day

  1. Ask the moment it works, not at the door — while they're still pleased.
  2. Do it on their computer. You've just fixed it, it's in front of you, and it's usually already signed in to Google. Type {{$d.Display}} into their browser and hand back the keyboard.

  4. Phone instead? Show this page on your phone — their camera scans it straight through.
  5. Not signed in to Google? Don't fight it. Say you'll send a link with the receipt, then tick Ask for a Google review when you mark the invoice paid.
